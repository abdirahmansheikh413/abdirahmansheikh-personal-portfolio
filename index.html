I don't share this to be admired. I share it because it's the reason I build the way I do: patiently, with discipline, and with deep respect for the people the world tends to overlook. I learn differently — and that's exactly why accessibility isn't a feature to me. It's personal.

Lead Developer · Full-Stack

Luxe Decor — Booking & Deposit System

Production website and booking system for a family-run event decor business, live and taking real bookings. An instant quote calculator returns a price range with a line-item breakdown; an availability calendar blocks dates that are already booked; deposits are collected through Stripe Checkout; and a phone-friendly admin dashboard lets the owners change booking status, block dates, and export to CSV. Quotes are recomputed server-side, so a price submitted by the browser is never trusted. Mobile Lighthouse: 90–98 performance and 100 accessibility across every public page, with reduced-motion support throughout.
