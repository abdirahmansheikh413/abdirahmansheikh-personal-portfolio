Founder & Developer · Senior CapstoneIn development

Adaptive Learning OS — An AI system that answers "what should I do right now?"

The problem: students don't fail because they lack information — they fail because they don't know what to work on next. Planners are static, chatbots have no memory of your semester, and neither adapts to how a specific person learns.

What I'm building: not a chatbot with a syllabus attached. A system that reads course material, extracts real deadlines and workload, models the student's context in a database, and turns that into a plan that changes as the semester does. The guiding principle: the student shouldn't adapt to the software — the software should adapt to the student.

Syllabus→ Extract deadlines→ Model workload→ Build plan→ Recommend next step→ Track progress→ Adapt

Built

  • Product architecture & data model design
  • LLM API integration with structured outputs
  • Syllabus parsing into structured coursework

In development

  • Database-backed persistent student context
  • Workload modeling & adaptive planning logic
  • Next-action recommendation engine

Exploring

  • Tool-calling agents for calendar & task systems
  • RAG over course material
  • Proactive notifications with human approval
  • Accessibility & multilingual support

Why it matters to me: I'm building it for myself first. Every design decision starts from a real constraint I've actually hit as a student, not a feature list.
